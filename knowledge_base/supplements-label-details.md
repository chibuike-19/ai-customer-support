# Supplements label details

## How to use this section
These are the manufacturer's directions for the branded products we usually stock. Packs and strengths can change, so always remind the customer to follow the label on their own pack.
For anything not listed here, tell the customer to follow the directions on the pack, and offer to have a colleague confirm.

## Wellwoman Max (Vitabiotics)
A women's multivitamin in a 28-day triple pack: 28 micronutrient tablets, 28 Omega 3-6-9 capsules, and 28 calcium and vitamin D tablets.
Directions: one of each daily, with the main meal, on a full stomach. Swallow whole with water or a cold drink, not tea or coffee. Do not exceed the stated intake. No other multivitamin is needed.
Contains fish oil, soya, and a gelatin capsule (halal bovine source), so it is not vegetarian. Gluten free. Contains a small amount of caffeine from green tea and guarana.
Label cautions: not for children. Check with a doctor or pharmacist first if pregnant, breastfeeding, under medical supervision, or taking blood thinners (it contains vitamin K). Contains iron. Store below 25°C.

## Wellman Max (Vitabiotics)
A men's multivitamin in a 28-day triple pack: 28 micronutrient tablets, 28 Omega 3-6-9 capsules, and 28 calcium and vitamin D tablets.
Directions: one of each daily, with or right after a main meal. Swallow with water or a cold drink, not tea or coffee. Do not chew. Do not take on an empty stomach. No other multivitamin is needed.
Contains fish oil, soya, and a gelatin capsule (halal bovine source), so it is not vegetarian. Gluten free.
Label cautions: not for children. Check with a doctor or pharmacist first if under medical supervision. Store below 25°C.

## Wellwoman Original (Vitabiotics)
A women's daily multivitamin and mineral capsule, with evening primrose and starflower oil. Usually 30 capsules (one month).
Directions: one capsule daily with the main meal. Swallow with water or a cold drink, not tea or coffee. Take only on a full stomach.
Contains soya and a gelatin capsule. Gluten free.
Label cautions: not for children. Check with a doctor or pharmacist first if pregnant, breastfeeding, or taking blood thinners (it contains vitamin K). Contains iron. Store below 25°C.

## Centrum Performance
A daily multivitamin for active people, with ginkgo biloba.
Directions: one tablet daily with water, preferably with food.
Gluten free.

## Centrum Silver
A daily multivitamin made for adults aged 50 and over.
Directions: follow the label on the pack. A colleague can confirm if needed.

## Serious Mass (Optimum Nutrition)
A high-calorie weight gainer powder.
Directions: 2 heaping scoops blended with about 700 ml (24 fl oz) of water or milk. New users can start with half a serving a day for the first week.
Per serving with water: about 1,250 calories and 50 g protein.
Label cautions: not for under-18s, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Check with a doctor first if diabetic or taking medication.

## Rule 1 Mass Gainer
A high-calorie weight gainer powder.
Directions: blend 2 heaping scoops with 600 to 700 ml (20 to 24 fl oz) of water or milk. Use alongside regular meals, not instead of them.
Per serving: over 1,200 calories and 40 g protein, with 1 g creatine.

## Body Fortress Whey Protein
A whey protein powder.
Directions: 1 scoop in 180 to 240 ml of liquid, or 2 scoops in 400 to 470 ml. Stir, shake, or blend, then drink within 10 minutes.
Per scoop: 30 g protein.
Contains milk and soy.
Label cautions: not for under-18s, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Avoid with kidney disease.

## Lamosa Advanced Weight Gain
A 900 g vanilla weight gain drink powder for adults.
Directions from our listing: mix 5 tablespoons with warm water. Follow the label on the tin.

## Amino Energy (Optimum Nutrition)
An amino acid drink powder with caffeine.
Directions: 2 scoops (9 g) in 300 to 350 ml of water, stirred until dissolved.
Contains 100 mg of caffeine per serving. Do not exceed 8 scoops in 24 hours.
Label cautions: not for under-18s, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Avoid other caffeine while using it. May cause a harmless tingling feeling.

## Lipo 6 Black (Nutrex)
A caffeine-based fat burner capsule.
Directions on the common version: 3 capsules twice a day, in the morning and afternoon, between meals. Not within 6 hours of sleep. Use for up to 2 months, then take a 1-month break.
Contains 220 mg of caffeine per serving.
Label cautions: not for under-18s, during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for anyone sensitive to caffeine. Do not combine with coffee, energy drinks, or other stimulants. Several versions exist, so the pack label must be followed.

## Move Free Advanced (Schiff)
A joint supplement with glucosamine and chondroitin.
Directions: 2 tablets once a day with a meal, or 1 tablet twice a day with meals.
Contains shellfish (shrimp, crab, lobster, crayfish).
Label cautions: not for under-18s, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

## Efamol Evening Primrose Oil
Pure evening primrose oil capsules.
Directions: 1 x 1000 mg capsule daily with food, or 2 x 500 mg capsules. The label suggests a higher amount for the first 12 weeks.
Contains gelatin.

## Super Collagen + C (NeoCell)
Hydrolyzed collagen types 1 and 3 with vitamin C, from bovine (beef) sources.
Directions: 2 tablets three times daily (6 tablets a day) for adults.
Gluten free.

## L'il Critters Gummy Vites
A children's multivitamin gummy.
Directions: ages 2 to 3, 1 gummy daily. Ages 4 and over, 2 gummies daily.
Contains gelatin and coconut.
Label cautions: not for children under 2 because of choking risk. Keep out of reach of children.

## Melatonin (Puritan's Pride)
A sleep aid tablet. The 5 mg tablet is the common strength.
Directions: adults take one tablet at bedtime.
Label cautions: not for under-18s, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Do not drive or drink alcohol after taking it. Use for up to two months, then take a one-week break.

## OvaBoost for Women (Fairhaven Health)
A women's fertility supplement with myo-inositol, folate, CoQ10, and a small amount of melatonin.
Directions: 4 capsules daily, all at once or split into two doses, with food. One bottle of 120 capsules lasts one month.
Any question about trying to conceive or pregnancy goes to a colleague.

## CountBoost for Men (Fairhaven Health)
A men's fertility supplement.
Directions: 2 capsules daily in divided doses with meals. One bottle of 60 capsules lasts one month.
Our Jiji listing is named "Count Boost Motility Boost". MotilityBoost is a separate product from the same brand, so a colleague should confirm which one the customer is buying.

## Myo and D-Chiro Inositol (Intimate Rose)
A women's inositol supplement with vitamin D3 and ashwagandha.
Directions: 4 capsules daily with a meal. People with a sensitive stomach can take 2 capsules twice a day.
Vegetarian capsules. Made from corn.

## Women's Care Once Daily Probiotics (Garden of Life)
A women's probiotic, 50 billion CFU per capsule.
Directions: 1 capsule daily, with or without food. Shelf stable, no need to refrigerate.
Not intended for children.

## Women's Probiotic + Cranberry (Nature's Truth)
A women's probiotic with cranberry extract.
Directions: 1 capsule daily, preferably with a meal. No need to refrigerate.
Label cautions: not for under-18s.

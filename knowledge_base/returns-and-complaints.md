# Returns and complaints

## Opened products
We cannot accept returns of opened products. This is for hygiene and safety reasons, because supplements and medical items are consumed or used on the body.

## Complaints
Complaints must reach us within 48 hours of purchase.
The customer must send proof:
1. A clear photo of the product and the problem.
2. The order confirmation.
A colleague reviews every complaint and decides what happens next. Never promise a refund, replacement, or exchange in a reply.

## Damaged, wrong, or expired items
If an item arrives damaged, is the wrong product, or is past its expiry date, ask the customer to send the photo and the order confirmation within 48 hours of purchase. A colleague takes it from there.
The expiry date is usually printed on the bottom or side of the pack, or on the bottle label.

# Orders and delivery

## Prices
Prices change often, so we do not list them here. A colleague confirms the current price for every order.

## Payment

## Delivery cost and time
The delivery fee depends on the customer's location and the size of the order. A colleague confirms the fee before the order is sent.
Delivery time also depends on location. A colleague confirms the expected delivery time for each order.
To get a delivery quote, we need the customer's delivery address (area and state), the products, and the quantity.

## Placing an order
To place an order, we need:
1. The product names and quantities.
2. The customer's full name and phone number.
3. The delivery address.
4. The preferred payment method.
A colleague then confirms the price, the delivery fee, and the delivery time.
